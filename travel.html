Flying In

The closest airport is Daniel K. Inouye International Airport (HNL) in Honolulu, about a 15–20 minute drive from the venue. Most major U.S. carriers fly directly into HNL, and many airlines offer nonstop routes from the West Coast and beyond.

Because March is a popular travel month in Hawai’i, we recommend booking flights and rental cars a few months in advance.

Getting Around

  • Renting a car is the easiest way to explore Oahu at your own pace.
  • Rideshare (Uber/Lyft) is widely available in Honolulu and Waikiki.
  • TheBus, Honolulu's public bus system, is affordable and covers most of the island.
  • On-site parking will be available at the venue — details to follow.
Where to Stay

Suggested Accommodations

Waikiki

Classic Beachfront

Waikiki has the widest range of hotels and resorts, about a 20–25 minute drive to the venue, plus easy beach access and nightlife.

Downtown / Nu’uanu

Closest to the Venue

Staying downtown or near Nu’uanu puts you within about 10–15 minutes of Natsunoyah Tea House, with a quieter, more local feel.

Ala Moana

Shopping & Central

A good middle ground between Waikiki and downtown, close to Ala Moana Center and an easy drive to the venue.

Weather & Packing

Late March in Honolulu is warm and pleasant, typically in the high 70s to low 80s°F (25–28°C), with the occasional passing shower. Pack light, breathable layers, a light rain jacket, comfortable shoes for outdoor activities, and something a little dressier for the wedding itself.
