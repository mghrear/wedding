RSVP Form Fields

Copy these fields into a new Google Form (forms.google.com):

  • Full Name(s) — short answer
  • Will you be attending? — multiple choice: Joyfully Accepts / Regretfully Declines
  • Number of Guests Attending — multiple choice or short answer
  • Meal Choice — multiple choice: Chicken / Fish / Vegetarian / Vegan (per guest, or duplicate this question)
  • Dietary Restrictions or Allergies — short answer (optional)
  • Song Request — short answer (optional)
  • Message for the Couple — paragraph (optional)
  • Email Address — short answer, for confirmation/updates
