While You're on O’ahu

Things To Do

We hope you'll make a trip of it! Here are a few of our favorite ways to spend time on O’ahu before or after the wedding.

Beach

Waikiki Beach

The classic O’ahu beach day — swimming, surf lessons, and sunset walks along the shore.

Hike

Diamond Head Crater

A short, popular hike with sweeping views over Honolulu and the coastline. Book tickets ahead of time.

History

Pearl Harbor National Memorial

A moving, important stop — reserve free tickets to the USS Arizona Memorial in advance.

Snorkel

Hanauma Bay

A protected marine reserve with calm, clear water — one of the best snorkeling spots on the island.

Day Trip

North Shore

Famous surf breaks, food trucks, and the charming town of Hale’iwa — about an hour's drive.

Culture

Chinatown & Downtown Honolulu

Galleries, local eateries, and historic architecture, just minutes from the venue.

Garden

Lyon Arboretum & Foster Botanical Garden

Lush, quiet spots to wander — a nice complement to our garden-inspired wedding.

Food

Local Favorites

Try poke bowls, malasadas, shave ice, and a classic plate lunch while you're here.
