While You're on O’ahu

Things To Do

Beach

Waikiki Beach

The classic O’ahu beach day — swimming, surf lessons, and sunset walks along the shore.

Snorkel

Hanauma Bay

A protected marine reserve with calm, clear water — one of the best snorkeling spots on the island.

Day Trip

North Shore

Famous surf breaks, food trucks, and the charming town of Hale’iwa — about an hour's drive.

Culture

Chinatown & Downtown Honolulu

Galleries, local eateries, and historic architecture, just minutes from the venue.

Garden

Lyon Arboretum & Foster Botanical Garden

Lush, quiet spots to wander — a nice complement to our garden-inspired wedding.

Food

O’Kims

1028 Nuuanu Ave, Honolulu, HI 96817

Really amazing Korean fusion food. Please say hi to Hyun for us! She was Ama’s boss but like a mom to her.
